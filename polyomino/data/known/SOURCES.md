# Data provenance

Retrieved / generated: 2026-10-02.

## External factual sources

- `polyomino-counts.json`: count reference from https://polyominoes.org/count. Used to check the locally generated free/holeless catalog counts.
- `heesch-n7-n10.json`: canonicalized factual records from Craig S. Kaplan's published *Heesch Numbers of Unmarked Polyforms* dataset, https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/~csk/heesch/. This bundle contains the complete published non-tiler lists represented here for orders 7–10: 3, 20, 198, and 1390 records respectively. `Hc` forbids holes in the outermost corona; `Hh` permits them. Records were matched to local canonical IDs.
- `tiling-facts.json`: small curated facts checked against https://polyominoes.org/data; currently includes the U-heptomino torus regression fact.
- Joseph Myers' https://www.polyomino.org.uk/mathematics/polyform-tiling/ is a research reference for translation/180°/isohedral/anisohedral/non-tiler classifications. Its large tiling lists are **not copied wholesale** into this bundle.

The execution sandbox did not permit arbitrary direct external file downloads during this revision. External factual datasets included here were normalized from material available during research; the large shape/feature/periodic databases below are generated locally instead of mirroring third-party databases.

## Locally generated databases

- `../catalog/free-holeless-n1..12.json`: 81,265 canonical free, connected, holeless polyominoes, generated by iterative cell growth, D4 canonicalization and hole filtering. Counts are checked against the external count reference.
- `../features/shape-features-n1..12.json`: compact per-shape symmetry and geometric features. Not loaded on ordinary startup.
- `periodic-seeds-n1..10.json`: bounded exact torus seeds produced by the bundled DLX solver. A missing record is **not** a non-tiling certificate.
- `periodic-index.json`: compact lookup index derived from the seed files for catalog badges.
- `complex-periodic-leaderboard.json`: metrics recomputed from all currently cached exact seeds and sorted by the local complexity heuristic; not a global mathematical ranking.
- `complexity-index.json`: compact subset/index of the leaderboard for catalog display and `★ / MU` search.
- `rectangle-seed-index.json`: exact cached torus seeds whose recorded placements do not wrap across the torus cut, hence also form a finite rectangle tiling.
- `bounded-classification-index.json`: merges cached positive witnesses and matched published non-tiler records; every omitted case is explicitly unknown.
- `catalog-statistics.json`, `heesch-index.json`, `manifest.json`: derived indexes and integrity metadata.
